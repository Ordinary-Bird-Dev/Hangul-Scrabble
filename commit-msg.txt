Korean TTS, tutorial level, learning-hub navigation, and unified GameScene layout

Speech:
- KoreanTTS.jslib + SpeechManager wrap the Web Speech API, with an iOS
  unlock primer fired from a user gesture.
- Hint now speaks the word instead of printing romanization. First press
  costs 50 points; every later press for the same word replays free until
  it's solved.
- Correct words are read back in all three modes, not just Classic.

Tutorial:
- TutorialController scripts ClassicModeController through five
  one-syllable lessons (개 코 눈 물 별) inside GameScene rather than a
  duplicate scene, so layout fixes only ever happen once.
- No countdown, no score, no gauge. Skip exits to the title; finishing
  all five goes straight into a real round.

Navigation:
- Title screen is down to Play + How to Play. How to Play is now the
  learning hub and owns the links to Hangul Basics and the Tutorial.
- InfoPageController routes Back contextually so Hangul Basics returns to
  How to Play instead of stranding the player at the title.

Layout:
- GameScene rebuilt to the Unified mockup: 968-wide content band,
  22/9 vertical rhythm, clue card, and edge-to-edge tile tray.
- ResultScene mascot fixed to 900x660 with PreserveAspect, matching the
  GameScene prefab it had drifted from.

Fixes:
- Word bar clears on a wrong word in Zen and Word Hunt, not just Classic.
- Clue banner defers its next word until the meaning card has gone, so
  the two no longer change under each other.
- Reset button has a sound.
- Progress gauge tracks words completed instead of time remaining.
- Replaced GameObject.Find with inactive-inclusive lookups in GameManager
  and InfoPageController.

WebGL:
- HangulFit template sets the canvas size from JS with !important and
  re-asserts on resize, since Unity's loader writes inline styles that
  beat any stylesheet rule.

How to Play and Hangul Basics copy updated.
